About VOIS
Vodafone Intelligent Solutions (VOIS) is a strategic arm of Vodafone Group, delivering technology, business transformation, and digital solutions across multiple international markets.

At VOIS, we leverage data, technology, and innovation to create meaningful business impact, improve operational efficiency, and enable smarter decision-making.

We are looking for a motivated and analytical Data Analyst to join our team in Egypt and contribute to transforming complex datasets into actionable insights that support business and operational objectives.
The Role
As a Data Analyst, you will work closely with cross-functional teams to collect, process, analyze, and interpret data. You will help identify trends, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), and develop insightful dashboards and reports that support strategic and operational decisions.

You will also contribute to improving data quality, automating reporting processes, and translating business requirements into effective analytical solutions.
Key Responsibilities
- Collect, clean, validate, and analyze large datasets from multiple sources.
- Develop and maintain interactive dashboards and reports using Power BI.
- Write SQL queries to extract, transform, and manipulate data.
- Use Python to automate repetitive analytical tasks and support data processing.
- Identify patterns, trends, and anomalies to generate actionable business insights.
- Monitor business performance through KPIs and performance reports.
- Collaborate with business stakeholders to understand analytical requirements.
- Ensure data accuracy, consistency, and reliability across reporting systems.
- Present analytical findings and recommendations to relevant stakeholders.
- Contribute to continuous improvement of reporting and data analysis processes.
Required Qualifications & Skills
Education

- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Commerce, Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, Statistics, or a related field.

Technical Skills
- Good understanding of Microsoft Excel, including Pivot Tables, lookup functions, and data manipulation.
- Working knowledge of SQL and relational databases.
- Practical knowledge of Power BI and dashboard development.
- Basic to intermediate Python programming skills.
- Understanding of data cleaning, data transformation, and data visualization.
- Familiarity with statistical concepts and analytical techniques.
Professional Skills
- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
- Attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy.
- Good communication and presentation skills.
- Ability to translate business questions into analytical solutions.
- Effective time management and ability to work collaboratively.
- Good command of written and spoken English.
Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with Google BigQuery or other cloud-based data platforms.
- Familiarity with ETL processes and data integration.
- Knowledge of Python libraries such as Pandas and NumPy.
- Understanding of business intelligence concepts and KPI reporting.
- Previous academic or personal projects involving data analysis.
What We Offer
- Opportunity to work in a dynamic, technology-driven environment.
- Exposure to real-world business intelligence and data analytics practices.
- Collaboration with multidisciplinary and international teams.
- Continuous learning and professional development opportunities.
- Access to modern analytical tools and technologies.
- An inclusive working environment that values innovation and collaboration.
